How I work

  • 4 live hours a day, during your business hours, for calls, quick replies and anything urgent. I reply within 1 hour during live hours.
  • Focus work outside that window, so records, books and reports are ready when you start your day.
  • One place for everything: tasks, updates, documents and file links on a shared Trello board.
  • A short end-of-day summary: what's done, what's waiting on you, what's next.
  • New batch tasks start small: I do the first few, you confirm, then I finish the rest.
  • Already using other software? I'll work inside your tools and get up to speed on them.

How working together starts

  1. Book a short call

    Pick a time that works for you. We talk for 20 to 30 minutes about your business, what's piling up, and the tools you use. No pitch, just questions.

  2. Get a simple plan

    Within two business days, I send a short summary: what I'd take off your plate first, the goals for our first two weeks, and a simple agreement.

  3. Start a 2-week paid trial

    Same hours, same rate, no long-term commitment. In the first weeks, most of my hours overlap with your workday while I learn your business.

  4. Decide together

    At the end of two weeks, we check the goals. Continue, adjust, or end it cleanly; it's your call.

Why a 4-hour daily minimum?

I can't run the office side of a business I don't understand. Four hours a day gives me enough time with your jobs, tools and people to learn how your business actually runs, so I can stop waiting for instructions and start keeping things moving on my own. In the first weeks, most of those hours overlap with your workday while I learn. After that, part of my work moves outside your hours, so things are done before you start your day.

Pricing

A 2-week paid trial, then a monthly plan paid upfront. Pricing depends on scope; we'll agree on it after our call.
